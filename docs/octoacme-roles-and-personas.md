# OctoAcme Personas

**Version:** 2.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-02-08  
**Change Summary:** Added UX/UI Designer, Technical Writer, Security Lead, Support Engineer, and Business Analyst personas to address cross-functional team needs.

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/UI Designer

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers ensure accessible and user-friendly design through wireframes, user flows, and high-fidelity prototypes. They bridge user needs with product requirements and technical implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Design user interfaces and interactions that meet accessibility standards
- Partner with Product Managers to refine requirements and validate design decisions
- Support Developers with visual assets, design systems, and implementation guidance
- Gather and act on feedback from usability tests and user research
- Maintain design consistency across features and product areas

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce friction in user workflows
- Ensure design-to-implementation consistency

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Product Managers and Developers
- Usability testing sessions and research findings
- Design specifications and component documentation
- Weekly syncs during active design phases

### Interactions with Core Roles
- **Product Managers:** Collaborate during early requirement definition and validate designs against user needs and business goals
- **Developers:** Provide design specs, assets, and guidance during implementation; review front-end code for design adherence
- **Project Managers:** Coordinate design milestones and dependencies; communicate design risks or scope impacts

---

## Technical Writer

### Role Summary
Technical Writers deliver user and technical documentation that enables transparency and supportability for all project stakeholders. They translate complex technical concepts into clear, actionable content.

### Responsibilities
- Produce and maintain end-user help content, API guides, and developer documentation
- Coordinate with Developers and QA for technical accuracy and coverage
- Update documentation with each release and feature change
- Create onboarding guides and troubleshooting resources
- Ensure documentation accessibility and searchability

### Goals
- Enable self-service support and reduce support escalations
- Maintain accurate, up-to-date technical content
- Improve developer and user experience through clear documentation

### Typical Communication
- Documentation reviews with Developers and Product Managers
- Content planning sessions aligned to release cycles
- Feedback analysis from Support Engineers and users
- Style guide and template updates

### Interactions with Core Roles
- **Developers:** Partner for technical accuracy, API details, and implementation guidance
- **Support Engineers:** Identify recurring help topics and documentation gaps from support tickets
- **Product Managers:** Align documentation priorities with feature releases and user needs

---

## Security Lead

### Role Summary
Security Leads ensure product security and regulatory compliance across all development stages. They provide security expertise, conduct risk assessments, and guide the team in implementing secure practices.

### Responsibilities
- Define security requirements and best practices for the project
- Lead threat modeling and vulnerability assessments
- Respond to security incidents and coordinate remediation
- Review code and architecture for security concerns
- Maintain security documentation and compliance artifacts

### Goals
- Prevent security vulnerabilities in production
- Ensure regulatory and compliance requirements are met
- Foster a security-aware development culture

### Typical Communication
- Security reviews during design and implementation phases
- Threat modeling sessions with engineering teams
- Incident reports and post-mortems
- Security training and awareness communications

### Interactions with Core Roles
- **Developers:** Advise on secure coding practices, conduct code reviews, and provide remediation guidance
- **Project Managers:** Support risk registers with security risks; communicate security-related timeline impacts
- **QA/Testing:** Collaborate on security-focused test scenarios and penetration testing

---

## Support Engineer

### Role Summary
Support Engineers interface between users and the product team post-release to enable rapid triage and resolution of issues. They are the voice of the customer within the engineering organization.

### Responsibilities
- Prepare support playbooks and user troubleshooting materials
- Capture feedback from live issues and escalate product bugs
- Help resolve post-launch technical and usability concerns
- Track support metrics and identify systemic issues
- Contribute to knowledge base and self-service resources

### Goals
- Minimize time to resolution for user issues
- Reduce repeat support tickets through documentation and fixes
- Provide actionable feedback to improve product quality

### Typical Communication
- Daily triage and ticket updates
- Bug reports and feature requests to Product team
- Support metrics reviews and trend analysis
- Collaboration on documentation improvements

### Interactions with Core Roles
- **Technical Writers:** Partner on documentation to address common support issues and gaps
- **Developers:** Report bugs with detailed reproduction steps; validate fixes in staging
- **Product Managers:** Close the loop on recurring problems; provide user insights to inform prioritization

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business requirements into actionable specifications and assist with acceptance criteria and post-release validation. They ensure alignment between business objectives and technical solutions.

### Responsibilities
- Gather and document business and user requirements
- Analyze operational processes and suggest improvements
- Support prioritization and user story validation with the team
- Create process flows and requirement specifications
- Validate delivered solutions against business needs

### Goals
- Ensure solutions align with business objectives
- Reduce rework through clear, accurate requirements
- Enable data-driven decision making

### Typical Communication
- Requirements gathering sessions with stakeholders
- User story refinement and backlog grooming
- Process mapping and documentation reviews
- Validation sessions with Product and Project teams

### Interactions with Core Roles
- **Product Managers:** Partner on requirement definition, market analysis, and prioritization decisions
- **Project Managers:** Provide requirement clarity and support scope management
- **Developers:** Translate business requirements into technical specifications; validate acceptance criteria
